Frequently asked questions

Good to know before you swap.

Can Lucky Loki swap faces in both photos and videos?

Yes. Lucky Loki is an AI face swap app for iPhone and Android that works with photos and short video clips. Add a source face, choose your target content, and the app creates the face-swapped result.

Does a face swap change hair, skin tone, clothing, or the background?

Lucky Loki replaces the selected face. The hairstyle, visible skin and body, clothing, accessories, pose, and background come from the target photo or video.

How can I get more realistic AI face swap results?

Start with a clear, front-facing source image taken in even lighting. A visible target face at a similar angle usually produces a more natural-looking face swap.

Can I use AI face swaps for commercial projects?

Yes. Lucky Loki can be used for lawful commercial and professional projects, including creative production, branded content, localization, and adapting existing footage. You must have the necessary rights and permissions for the faces and content you use, and an active subscription.

Are my photos and videos used to train AI models?

No. User content is not used to train, fine-tune, improve, or evaluate AI models. Source face images you save remain in your gallery until you remove them. Target photos, videos, and generated results are processed temporarily for creation and delivery. See the Privacy Policy for details.

Is Lucky Loki free to use?

Creating face swaps requires Lucky Loki Pro. You can start with a free trial. A paid quarterly subscription begins automatically when the trial ends unless you cancel beforehand. The App Store or Google Play shows the applicable price and final terms before confirmation.
