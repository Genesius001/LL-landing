FAQ

What is Lucky Loki?

An AI face swap app for photos and short video clips. You give it a photo of a face and a piece of content you have the right to use, and it replaces the face in that content. It runs on iPhone, iPad and Android.

Does it work on videos, or only photos?

Both, with the same two steps — you can swap faces in photos and in short video clips. Video is not a separate mode: pick a clip instead of a photo and the rest of the flow is the same, with no editing skills needed.

How do I get the best results?

Use a clear, front-facing selfie in even light, with nothing covering the face — no sunglasses, no hand, no hard shadow across one side. In the target photo or clip, a face at a similar angle to your selfie gives the cleanest swap.

Does it work on both iPhone and Android?

Yes. The app is on the App Store for iPhone and iPad, and on Google Play for Android. It also runs on Apple silicon Macs and Apple Vision Pro.

Do I need permission to use someone else's face?

Yes — explicit permission from that person, for that use. There is no exception for friends, colleagues or public figures. Our Acceptable Use Policy sets out what that means in practice.

Is AI face swap legal?

In most places, yes — when it is done with consent and the result is disclosed as AI-generated. Without those two things it stops being lawful very quickly, and a growing number of countries and US states attach real penalties to synthetic imagery made without consent.

The rules we hold you to are in the Acceptable Use Policy; disclosure is covered on the AI Transparency page. This is general information, not legal advice.

What happens to the photos I upload?

What we collect, what we do with it, who processes it and how long it is kept is set out in our Privacy Policy. You can ask us to delete your data at any time.

What does Pro cost, and how do I cancel?

Lucky Loki Pro is an auto-renewing subscription with weekly, monthly and quarterly plans; the quarterly plan starts with a 3-day free trial. Prices are shown in the app and vary by country.

You cancel in your App Store or Google Play subscription settings, at least 24 hours before the current period ends. You do not need to contact us.

Responsible use

Consent first. Every single time.

Lucky Loki makes synthetic media — it shows something that did not happen. Two rules cover almost all of it: get permission from every person whose face you use, and tell your audience the result is AI-generated. Intimate content, content depicting minors, impersonation and harassment are never allowed. The detail lives in the policies.
