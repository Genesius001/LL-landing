Report a concern

Four routes, one page. Whichever applies, you do not need a Lucky Loki account and you do not need to be a customer.

Last updated: 2026-08-13

Read this first — it will save you time. Lucky Loki is a generator, not a hosting service. There is no feed, no gallery and no public profile: the app makes a file and hands it to the person who made it. That means we cannot remove content from anywhere except our own systems — not from Instagram, TikTok, X, Telegram, a website, a private message or someone's phone. Any service that tells you otherwise is describing something it cannot deliver.

What we can do is act on our side and give you something you can use elsewhere. The routes below include the mechanisms that do reach other platforms.

1. Intimate imagery of you, made without your consent

Write to us, and file with StopNCII at the same time

Describe the content and where you saw it. Do not attach the image. Put URGENT in the subject line — the link below does it for you. We aim to send a first reply within 48 hours.

info@lucky-loki.com

The mechanism that actually reaches platforms is not us. StopNCII.org creates a digital fingerprint of the image on your own device — the image itself never leaves your phone — and shares only that fingerprint with participating platforms, which then block matching uploads across all of them at once. If the person depicted is under 18, NCMEC Take It Down does the same, and you should also contact your local police: this is a criminal offence in most countries.

If you are reporting imagery of someone else who is under 18, use the NCMEC CyberTipline. For support and guidance from people whose job this is, the Cyber Civil Rights Initiative runs a helpline.

2. Privacy and data requests

Your data, or your face in someone else's upload

Access, correction, deletion, objection, portability — and requests from people who never used Lucky Loki but believe their image was processed by it. Tell us what you are asking for; we will tell you what we hold and what we can do.

info@lucky-loki.com

How we handle personal data, and how to complain to a supervisory authority if you are unhappy with our answer, is set out in our Privacy Policy.

3. Copyright and other intellectual property

Your work used without permission

Include: identification of the work, identification of the material you say is infringing and where it is, your contact details, a statement that you believe in good faith the use is not authorized, a statement that the information is accurate, and your signature — electronic is fine.

info@lucky-loki.com

4. Everything else

Rule violations, questions, law enforcement

Anything that breaks our Acceptable Use Policy, questions about how the product works, and requests from law enforcement or a court.

info@lucky-loki.com

What we do with a report

Within the limits described at the top of this page:

  • We delete anything connected to the report that we hold.
  • Where we can identify the account behind it, we terminate that account and refuse further service.
  • We confirm in writing what we did, so far as we are legally permitted to say, in a form you can forward to a platform or to law enforcement. A documented report is taken more seriously than an unsupported one, and this is often the most useful thing we can give you.
  • We preserve records where we are required or permitted to, and respond to lawful requests from police, prosecutors or a court.

Where a report concerns content we never processed, or an account we cannot identify, we will say so plainly rather than leave you waiting.

Reports made in bad faith

Filing a knowingly false report — to get someone's content removed, to harass them, or to test the process — is a breach of our Terms of Use, and depending on where you are it may be unlawful.
