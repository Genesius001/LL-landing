Privacy request

For access, correction, deletion, objection, or another privacy right, explain what you are requesting and provide information reasonably sufficient for verification. Do not send identity documents unless we ask for them.

Describe the concern and any information that may help us identify activity within our systems. Do not send illegal content or unnecessary intimate imagery. If someone is in immediate danger, contact local emergency services.

Copyright, likeness, or other rights

Identify the requesting authority or party, legal basis, requested records, relevant account or transaction information if available, and a contact for follow-up.
