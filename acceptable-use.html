Acceptable Use Policy

Lucky Loki lets you put a face where it was not. That is useful, and it is also easy to misuse. This page sets out what is allowed, what is not, and what happens when someone crosses the line.

The one rule everything else follows from: you must have the right to the content you upload, and explicit permission from every identifiable person whose face appears in what you create. If you cannot point to that permission, do not make the swap.

1. Scope

This Acceptable Use Policy (the “Policy”) governs your use of Lucky Loki, including the iOS and Android applications and any related service or interface we make available (together, the “Services”).

It supplements our Terms of Use and our Privacy Policy. Where this Policy is stricter than the Terms of Use on a particular point, this Policy applies. Capitalized terms not defined here have the meaning given in the Terms of Use.

By using the Services you agree to this Policy. If you do not agree, do not use the Services.

2. Consent and rights in the content

Before you upload anything or generate anything, you must be able to answer yes to all of the following.

  • You have the rights to the source material. You own the photo or video, or you have a license that covers this use. Material scraped from social media, streaming services, stock libraries you have not licensed, or search results does not qualify.
  • Every identifiable person in the result has agreed to it. This means the person whose face you are adding and the person whose face you are replacing, where either is identifiable. Consent must be specific to this kind of use — someone agreeing to be photographed is not someone agreeing to be placed into synthetic media.
  • You can evidence that consent. If we ask you to demonstrate it, you should be able to. For commercial work, keep written releases.
  • The consent is current. Consent can be withdrawn. If a person tells you they no longer agree, stop using the material and remove what you have published.

Public figures

There is no public-figure exception. Politicians, actors, musicians, athletes, streamers and executives have the same rights here as anyone else, and in several jurisdictions they have additional statutory protection for their likeness. Do not use the Services to depict a public figure without that person's explicit permission.

People who cannot consent

Do not use the Services on the likeness of anyone who cannot give informed consent, including minors and deceased persons.

3. Disclosure of AI-generated content

Everything the Services produce is synthetic media: AI-generated or AI-manipulated content that depicts something which did not occur. When you share, publish, broadcast or supply that content to anyone else, you must make its synthetic nature clear to the audience.

This is not only good manners. In the European Union, Article 50 of the AI Act requires that content constituting a deepfake be disclosed. Several jurisdictions in the United States and elsewhere impose comparable duties, and every major social platform has its own labeling rules on top of the law. Meeting those obligations for content you publish is your responsibility.

See our AI Transparency page for more on this.

4. Prohibited uses

The following are prohibited without exception. There is no artistic, satirical, educational, research or “it was only a joke” carve-out for anything in this section.

✕ Sexual and intimate content

  • Nudity, partial nudity or sexually explicit imagery involving any real person
  • Placing any person's likeness into sexual, intimate or suggestive content
  • Content intended for sexual gratification
  • Any attempt to use the Services, by any workaround or indirect means, to produce any of the above

✕ Minors

  • Content depicting a person under 18 other than yourself, unless you are their parent or legal guardian
  • Any sexual, suggestive or intimate content involving a minor, in any form, real or synthetic — without exception
  • Content designed to appear to depict a minor in a sexual or suggestive context

✕ Deception and impersonation

  • Impersonating a real person to obtain money, data, access or advantage
  • Fraud, scams and social-engineering of any kind
  • Defeating or attempting to defeat identity verification, liveness checks, KYC or biometric authentication
  • Fabricated evidence, false documentation, fake news footage

✕ Harm to people

  • Harassment, bullying, stalking, intimidation or humiliation
  • Content that degrades a person or group on the basis of a protected characteristic
  • Threats, incitement to violence, glorification of violence
  • Content designed to damage a person's reputation or livelihood

✕ Politics and public interest

  • Political advertising, campaign material and electioneering
  • Content depicting candidates, officials or public bodies
  • Disinformation about elections, public health, emergencies or public safety
  • Impersonating a government, agency or official

✕ Abuse of the Services

  • Removing, obscuring or interfering with any label, watermark or provenance information that a file carries
  • Reselling or white-labeling output as your own service without a written agreement
  • Automated access, scraping or reverse engineering outside a documented interface
  • Uploading malware or attempting to disrupt the Services

Reminder about the “entertainment purposes” disclaimer. Labelling something as a joke, a prank, a parody or as being for entertainment only does not make a prohibited use permitted, and it will not protect you legally. Non-consensual intimate imagery in particular carries criminal liability in many jurisdictions.

5. Commercial and professional use

If you use the Services for advertising, marketing, client work, broadcast, publishing or any other commercial purpose, the following apply in addition to everything above.

  • Written releases. Obtain and retain a signed release from every person whose likeness appears in the output. The release must describe the synthetic use specifically, including the territories, media and duration it covers.
  • Chain of rights. Confirm you hold the rights to the underlying footage, music, sets, trade marks and any other protected element in the frame.
  • Disclosure in the finished asset. Label the published asset as AI-generated in the form required by the platform, the market and applicable law.
  • Performer protections. Where the person depicted is a performer, check whether local law or a collective agreement requires separate consent, representation or compensation for a digital replica. Several jurisdictions now do.
  • Sector rules. Financial services, healthcare, pharmaceuticals, gambling and children's products carry their own advertising restrictions. Synthetic media does not exempt you from them.

6. Age requirements

You must be at least 16 years old to use the Services. Where the law of your country sets a higher minimum age for entering into this agreement, or a higher age of consent for the processing of your personal data, that higher age applies instead.

The Services are not directed to anyone under 16, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from anyone under 16. If we become aware that an account belongs to someone below the applicable minimum age, we close it and delete the data associated with it.

Separately, and regardless of your own age, two rules apply to who may appear in what you create:

  • You may not create content depicting a person under 18 other than yourself, unless you are that person's parent or legal guardian and the content is ordinary, non-commercial family material.
  • No content depicting a minor may be sexual, suggestive or intimate in any way, in any context, ever. There is no exception to this, and it applies to content depicting yourself.

7. Enforcement

We may take any of the following steps where we believe this Policy has been breached, in a manner we consider proportionate to the breach.

  • Refuse or stop processing a request
  • Remove content stored in connection with the Services
  • Suspend or terminate your access, permanently and without refund
  • Report the matter to law enforcement or another competent authority
  • Preserve relevant records where we are required or permitted to do so

Serious breaches — in particular anything in the sexual content, minors, or deception categories — result in permanent termination on the first occurrence. We do not issue warnings for those.

If we act against your account, we will tell you the reason. If you believe we got it wrong, reply to that message and we will review the decision.

Nothing in this section limits any other remedy available to us or to a person affected by your conduct.

8. Reporting a violation

Anyone can report a suspected violation, whether or not they use Lucky Loki. You do not need an account, and you do not need to identify yourself beyond what is necessary for us to act.

Please note what the Services are: a generator, not a hosting service. We can delete what we hold and terminate the account behind a violation, but we cannot remove content that has been published elsewhere. The Report a concern page explains this and points to the mechanisms that do reach other platforms.
